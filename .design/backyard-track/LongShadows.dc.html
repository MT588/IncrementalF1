Option F — Long shadows

Same yard, late in the afternoon. The sun sits low off the top right, the shed throws its shadow right across the bottom straight, and the light gives the panel real depth. The catch is the fixed sun: the bike keeps the same shadow all the way round, which is a small lie you notice most on the far side.
